✦ Minting

How to mint

1
Get HYPE

Bridge or buy HYPE on Hyperliquid. You need at least 0.077 HYPE per NFT plus a small amount for gas.

2
Connect wallet

Visit hyptoadz.xyz and click "Connect Wallet". MetaMask and Rabby are supported. The site will prompt you to add HyperEVM automatically.

3
Select quantity

Choose 1–5 NFTs per transaction. Each costs 0.077 HYPE and includes 27,855 $TOADZ.

4
Confirm transaction

Approve the transaction in your wallet. Gas is minimal on HyperEVM (~$0.01).

5
Receive NFT + $TOADZ

NFT and $TOADZ arrive in your wallet instantly. Both are locked for transfers until mint ends and LP is added.

Why minting is almost free

When you mint, you pay 0.077 HYPE and receive 27,855 $TOADZ. Here is exactly why those $TOADZ are worth more than what you paid.

✦ TL;DR: LP pool creates a fair listing price. Your $TOADZ at listing is worth more than your mint cost.

The math

LP pool = ~340 HYPE + 100,000,000 $TOADZ Listing price = 340 HYPE × live HYPE price ÷ 100,000,000 Value per mint = 27,855 × listing price Mint cost = 0.077 HYPE NFT effective cost = ~$0.99 profit ✦

48-hour fair launch lock

All NFTs and $TOADZ tokens are non-transferable during the 48-hour mint window. This ensures a fair launch — no one can sell before liquidity is created.

Buy/sell tax

A 5% tax applies to all DEX buys and sells. Wallet-to-wallet transfers are not taxed.

Tax only activates after setDexPair() is called post-LP creation. System contracts (mint, staking) are excluded from tax.

Liquidity pool

The LP is created on HyperSwap V2 using HYPE + 100M $TOADZ. LP tokens are burned to 0xDead — permanently locking the liquidity.

🔒 LP tokens are burned at creation. No one — including the team — can ever remove the liquidity. This is enforced by the smart contract with no admin override.
