FAQ

Do I need to be a Hypurr holder to mint? ↓
No. Anyone can mint during the 48h public window. Hypurr holders get a free airdrop automatically — the public mint is open to all.
When does the mint open? ↓
Announced on Twitter/X @HyptoadzNFT. The Hypurr airdrop is distributed automatically before the public mint opens.
What happens if mint doesn't sell out? ↓
Unsold mint rewards (180M $TOADZ) flow into the NFT staking pool, increasing rewards for holders.
Can the team rug pull? ↓
No. LP tokens are burned to 0xDead by the smart contract. No team tokens, no pre-mine, no admin override.
How do I stake my NFT or $TOADZ? ↓
After mint ends and LP is created, go to the staking page. Stake NFTs with optional lock periods, or stake $TOADZ tokens with no lock required.
Where can I trade $TOADZ? ↓
HyperSwap V2 on HyperEVM. Goes live automatically after the 48h mint window. NFTs trade on DripTrade and other HyperEVM marketplaces.
What wallets are supported? ↓
MetaMask and Rabby. Any EVM wallet supporting custom networks works. Site auto-prompts to add HyperEVM (chain ID 999).
